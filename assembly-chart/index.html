How this tool works

A quick guide to the assembly organization chart

Two separate things

The personnel master is your list of people (the contacts). Assignments only point to a person — so editing someone once updates every position they hold.

Personnel master (contacts)

  • Click Personnel: n at the top (or Personnel master… inside the editor) to open the contact list — a full table in its own window, sorted A–Z by last name.
  • Use + Person to add someone, the search box to find them, and Edit on any row to change their details. The Roles column shows how many positions each person holds.
  • Remove deletes a contact — blocked while they are still assigned somewhere, so you never lose a name that is in use.
  • Import roster… / Export roster at the bottom move the people list (no assignments) in and out as JSON.
  • Tick Regular Pioneer where it applies. Phone numbers link to WhatsApp; e-mails open your mail app.

Assigning positions

  • Open Edit data. Under Oversight assignments and Departments, each field is a single search-and-pick box: click it to see everyone, or type a few letters to narrow it down, then click a match (or press Enter to take the top one).
  • The small i button on any row shows that person’s full stored details.
  • Next to each field, + creates a brand-new person and assigns them right there, and ✎ edits the currently assigned person — no detour through the personnel master needed.
  • Expand all / Collapse all at the top of this panel open or close every section — top-level tabs and nested ones — in one click.

Reusing a person: everyone lives once in the Personnel master. To put the same brother in another department, open that assignment’s Person field and start typing his name — pick him from the list and all his data (congregation, phone, e-mail, birth date) comes along automatically. Edit him once and every assignment updates. Use the + next to a Person field only for someone genuinely new.

Dates of birth accept a full date (e.g. 04.05.2001) or just a year (2001) — type it however you like.

Reordering: every assignment and every top-block responsibility has ↑/↓ buttons, so a newly added overseer can be moved above the assistants. Departments reorder the same way.

Bold overseers in Edit data → Document settings toggles bold weight for overseer, crew captain and coordinator rows — assistants always stay regular. The choice is saved with your backup.

Saving & sharing (JSON)

  • Import — one button; it auto-detects a roster file or a full backup.
  • Export personnel — the roster only (no assignments or settings). Safe to share.
  • Export backup — everything: settings, all assignments, departments and people. This is your complete save file.
  • Export CSV — a spreadsheet copy for Excel. Pick one row per assignment or one row per person, tick the columns you need. CSV is a one-way export — it cannot be imported back.

Printing

  • Print / PDF prints one A4 landscape sheet per page.
  • Compact print squeezes more rows onto each page.
  • Print with all details adds private e-mail, type, DOB, pioneer and notes under each name.

Privacy: private e-mail, birth date, pioneer status and notes are never printed unless you switch on “Print with all details”. A full backup does contain this private data — keep that file to yourself.
